# V19.5 — National Medication Catalog

Versioned, source-attributed reference catalog for medicines marketed in Morocco. It is not autonomous clinical decision support.

The Ministry of Health exposes a Medicines section covering national lists, pharmacovigilance, prices, alerts and guides. Its Directorate of Medicines and Pharmacy states that it manages a technical/economic medicine database and medicine authorisations and pricing.

Imports must use sources Tabib is legally permitted to reuse. Preserve source reference, retrieval date, effective period and content hash. Never scrape authenticated/private areas or bypass anti-bot controls.
