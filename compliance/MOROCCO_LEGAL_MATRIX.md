# Morocco Legal & Compliance Matrix — Tabib.ma

Status date: 2026-09-05. This is an engineering compliance-readiness document, not legal advice.

| Area | Main framework | Tabib impact | Evidence/control | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Personal data | Law 09-08 / CNDP | Patient, professional and account data | register, notices, access controls, retention | OPEN |
| Health data | CNDP health-data formalities | Medical documents, appointment context, telemedicine data | authorization dossier + DPIA + data map | **BLOCKER** |
| International transfers | CNDP transfer regime | Cloud/email/video/SMS/analytics providers | transfer matrix + contractual/legal mechanism | **BLOCKER** |
| Medical practice | Law 131-13 | Doctors, clinics, telemedicine | professional verification + medical governance | OPEN |
| Telemedicine | Law 131-13 + implementing texts | Video consultations | consent, confidentiality, provider review | **BLOCKER** |
| Cybersecurity | Law 05-20 + implementing framework where applicable | Security governance and audits | risk assessment, audit, incident process | OPEN / scope-dependent |
| E-commerce | Law 31-08 where applicable | Paid platform services | CGV, pricing, cancellation/refund information | OPEN |
| Trust services | Law 43-20 where applicable | Electronic signatures/consents | use qualified provider when legally required | OPEN |

## Required pre-production legal package

- CNDP formalities/authorization applicable to each processing operation.
- Data inventory and purposes.
- Data retention/deletion schedule.
- Data-subject rights workflow.
- Processor/subprocessor register and contracts.
- International-transfer analysis and CNDP procedure where applicable.
- DPIA/AIPD for high-risk health processing.
- Telemedicine legal validation.
- Final CGU/CGV/privacy/cookie notices reviewed by Moroccan counsel.

## Important distinction

Passing software tests does not equal CNDP approval, DGSSI qualification, medical authorization, or legal compliance certification.
