- Patient account management

## Necessity and proportionality

## Risks
- unauthorized disclosure of health data
- identity/account takeover
- excessive employee access
- provider/subprocessor compromise

- accidental retention
- malicious uploads

## Measures
- private storage
- RLS
- least privilege
- MFA for privileged roles
- encryption in transit/at rest
- audit logging
- retention/deletion
- incident response
- backup/restore
- security testing
