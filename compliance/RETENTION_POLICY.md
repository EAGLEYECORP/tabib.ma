# Data Retention & Deletion Policy — Template

Do not invent retention periods in code. The controller and Moroccan counsel must define periods per data category and legal obligation.

Required categories:
- account/profile
- appointments
- health documents
- document access logs
- audit logs
- payment/accounting records
- notification delivery records
- security logs
- support cases

For each category define: purpose, legal basis, retention period, archival rule, deletion/anonymization method, exceptions, responsible owner.
